# SGD-Net Science — Architecture Research for Scientific and Physical AI

**Model architecture research combining graph structure, temporal memory, geometric constraints, and adaptive computation.**

[Read online](https://honggui.github.io/sgd-net-science/) · [GitHub project](https://github.com/honggui/sgd-net-science) · [Start reading](guide/start-here.md) · [Full contents](SUMMARY.md)

This collection publishes SGD-Net papers, model components, mathematical analyses, and domain-specific construction proposals. These are research design documents; they do not include training code, model weights, or validated performance results.

## How to read <a href="#section-003" id="section-003"></a>

The reading site and GitBook sidebar organize chapters by topic. The two long documents, 13 and 36, are divided into separate chapters, with page contents and previous/next navigation. GitHub readers can browse the same documents from this page or SUMMARY.md.

The research content comes from 33 public documents. The registry contains 72 top-level units, 27 internal interfaces, and 56 units across seven domains. These configurations and counts do not represent implemented capabilities, nor must every task enable every component at once.
