# 9. Acceptance and Current Delivery Boundaries

Implementation must check entity/sample isolation, graph permutation consistency, geometric equivariance error, chirality policies, single updates of temporal states, missing-modality masks, common P/Q readouts, candidate isolation, actual deadline aborts, and predictions and costs on frozen data. Numerical unit tests cannot replace task evaluation.

This edition completes the architectural decomposition and domain construction descriptions. Many of the 72 units are optional or external modules; it cannot be claimed that 72 neural layers have been implemented. Novelty and effectiveness retain the research boundaries in documents 32–35; unknown data, label, or device conditions are recorded in domain documents as implementation dependencies.
