# 12. Capability Priority and Optional Compatibility Extensions (Revised 2026-09-12)

[47 Generality Review](../../47.md) is revised into an appendix on expressiveness boundaries and optional compatibility. The model's main objective is to achieve task-required capabilities; operator-by-operator reproduction of existing models with their original weights is not a prerequisite. Data-graph messaging differs from arbitrary program execution; limitations of fixed memory, symmetry, and first-order aggregation still require task-based counterexample checks.

U01–U08 are on-demand capability specifications, not implemented functions, and do not increase the 72 top-level units. Task bottlenecks trigger attention/order, addressable memory, higher-order representations, and probabilistic objectives; compiler and Aegis-X migration requirements primarily trigger complete computational-graph imports and conformance tests. Task models may be built directly without depending on compatibility models.

Implicit solving, generalized geometry, and persistent evolution are evaluated independently; retaining their definitions does not require every domain to enable them. They may be disabled when they fail to improve predetermined quality–cost metrics. Constraint modes are declared by task without requiring all physical dynamics to converge monotonically.
