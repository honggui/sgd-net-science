# Research status and contribution boundaries

This book publishes papers, equations, the complete model component catalog, and domain construction proposals. Its current status is research design. Experimental chapters describe validation that needs to be performed; they do not represent achieved training scores, actual scientific results, or a chip implementation.

Established research mechanisms and this project's candidate contributions must be evaluated separately. High-dimensional EGNNs, combinations of graphs and temporal models, implicit layers, and feedback memory all have related prior work. Combining modules does not by itself establish originality or superiority.

Code blocks are primarily design pseudocode. Class names in different chapters describe responsibilities; they do not guarantee that installable modules with those names exist. The original scope of access to technical references and its limitations remain in the text. Preparing this reading edition did not reproduce external results.

The book includes no third-party code, weights, data, or internal historical backups. See the NOTICE appendix for licensing status. The neural-behavior and signaling/synapse chapters describe general task designs, not collaboration, authorization, or endorsement by any individual or research institution.
